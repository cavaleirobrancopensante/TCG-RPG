## The Black Pact (B)

The Pact is the faith of the pact: everything it does has a price. It pays life for oversized creatures and cards, sacrifices its own followers to fuel its altars, and answers enemies with unconditional destruction, shrinking curses and hand attack. Its creatures carry deathtouch and dread instead of wings, and the Bloodied condition rewards the player who has already paid, until the debt finally comes due.

**Pact Initiate** · Common · Creature (Human Cultist) · B · 1/1 · When this dies, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
The cheapest body in the faith and the first piece of fodder for every sacrifice outlet. A 1/1 for one is below rate, but the drain on death means it is never a wasted card, whether it chump blocks or goes onto the Black Altar. The flavour states the Pact's logic plainly: even a follower's death belongs to his master.

**Pit Imp** · Common · Creature (Demon Imp) · B · 1/1 · Dread (can only be blocked by Pact creatures and Constructs).
A one-drop with dread, so it chips in a point every turn against any deck that has no Pact creatures or Constructs. One power keeps it harmless as a threat on its own, which is right for a common. Imps are the smallest servants of the pit, and the flavour keeps them petty and mean rather than charming.

**Blood-Oath Thrall** · Common · Creature (Human Cultist) · 1B · 3/2 · When this enters, you lose 1 life.
The pact in its simplest form: a 3/2 for two, a full point above rate, paid for with 1 life. It is the faith's honest aggressive common and an early step toward Bloodied. The fingertip of blood and the debt that came due later make clear that the deal is a trap.

**Tomb Crawler** · Common · Creature (Skeleton) · 1B · 1/1 · Deathtouch (any damage this deals to a creature destroys it).
A cheap deterrent. Nothing large wants to attack into a deathtouch blocker, so this 1/1 for two holds the ground far above its size. It shows the Pact's habit of destroying what is greater than itself, and as a Skeleton it is the cheapest body the bony host can field.

**Famished Ghoul** · Common · Creature (Zombie) · 1B · 2/2 · As long as you have 10 or less life (Bloodied), this gets +1/+1.
The common introduction to Bloodied: an on-rate 2/2 for two that becomes a 3/3 once you have paid enough life. It rewards the player who has been spending life freely without punishing anyone early. A ghoul that smells you better as you near the grave is exactly what the condition means.

**Hellmaw Hound** · Common · Creature (Demon Hound) · 2B · 2/2 · Dread (can only be blocked by Pact creatures and Constructs).
The common dread creature, a 2/2 for three with evasion priced like a flier. It replaces the old flying shade, since the Pact no longer flies, and gives the faith a steady evasive clock at common. Honest dogs fleeing from it while only the damned can stand in its way is dread told as flavour.

**Grave Chanter** · Common · Creature (Human Cultist) · 2B · 2/3 · When this enters, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
A 2/3 for three with a one point drain on arrival. The body is a little under rate and the drain makes it up, so it is a steady common that tips races and blocks small attackers. Singing hymns backwards is the traditional inversion of sacred worship and marks the cultist as plainly diabolic.

**Charnel Glutton** · Common · Creature (Zombie) · 2B · 2/2 · Sacrifice another Zombie: this gets +3/+3 until end of turn.
The Zombie sacrifice outlet (re-aimed in 1.27 with the new tribe sacrifice cost). A plain 2/2 for three that can eat a Restless Dead token or a doomed shambler to win a combat or push damage through. It only eats its own kind now, so the bonus grew from +2/+2 to +3/+3 to keep the card where it was. It teaches the faith's habit of spending its own creatures without being a combo engine, because the bonus only lasts the turn.

**Blight-Sower Cultist** · Common · Creature (Human Cultist) · 3B · 2/2 · When this enters, target enemy creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn.
The common shrink creature: a 2/2 for four that kills an x/2 or wins a combat when it arrives. The body is under rate because the effect is a real two for one against small creatures. A cultist scattering ash and calling it a blessing is a mockery of the farmer's prayer, which shows the faith for what it is.

**Ossuary Bone-Walker** · Common · Creature (Skeleton) · 3B · 3/3 · When this dies, create a 1/1 Restless Dead Zombie token.
A 3/3 for four, a point under rate, that leaves a Restless Dead behind. It trades for two cards and feeds sacrifice outlets, and it follows the rule that the Pact only raises the dead from death or sacrifice. Ossuaries are bone houses, and bones that keep walking after the body breaks are the faith's grim joke. Once a ghoul, it is now a Skeleton (renamed and redrawn in 1.25), so it feeds the Skeleton lords while its token feeds the Zombies.

**Pit-Chained Fiend** · Common · Creature (Demon) · 3B · 5/4 · When this enters, you lose 2 life.
The common top end for aggressive decks: a 5/4 for four, well above rate, for 2 life. It is the bigger brother of the Thrall and pushes its owner toward Bloodied. The chain is for show, and the flavour reminds the player that what really binds is the price.

**Barrow Wight** · Common · Creature (Skeleton) · 4B · 4/3 · Dread (can only be blocked by Pact creatures and Constructs).
A 4/3 with dread for five, the common finisher. It sits a point under a vanilla five-drop to pay for the evasion, which keeps it fair while still closing games against most faiths. Wights clutching their grave gold are old folklore, and its greed suits the Pact. It was always drawn as the crowned bones of a barrow king, so in 1.25 it honestly joins the Skeletons and gives them a common finisher.

**Grave Chill** · Common · Spell (Curse) · B · Target enemy creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn.
One mana for -2/-2 until end of turn: it kills x/2s outright or turns a combat. It is the cheapest removal in the faith and is limited by toughness, so big threats shrug it off. The cold that does not stay in the crypt is the shrink mechanic told as weather.

**Wither the Living** · Common · Spell (Curse) · 1B · Destroy target enemy creature with power 2 or less.
Unconditional destroy belongs to the Pact, but at common and two mana it is held to small creatures. It is efficient early removal that cannot answer bombs. The line about borrowed flesh is a reminder that the faith treats life as something to be repossessed.

**Bargain in Blood** · Common · Spell (Pact) · 1B · Draw two cards. You lose 2 life.
Draw two for two mana and 2 life, the Pact's only way to draw: by paying. It is a little cheaper than the plain draw spells of the Stoa because life is a real resource here. The scribe who never mentions the interest is the pact in miniature.

**Siphon of Ashes** · Common · Spell (Curse) · 1B · The enemy player loses 2 life and you gain 2 life.
A four point swing for two mana. It is reach for drain decks and does nothing to the board, which keeps it fair at common, and it lets a Bloodied player climb back out if he needs to. The cracked chalice deliberately profanes a sacred vessel, showing the Pact's mockery of holy things as the evil it is.

**Dread Whisper** · Common · Spell (Curse) · 2B · The enemy discards two cards at random.
Two cards at random for three mana, the classic common hand attack. It was a little cheap at two mana in the first set, so it moves to three and to common. Undoing every plan with a single word in the night is the tempter's work.

**Unquiet Grave** · Common · Spell (Necromancy) · 3B · Return target creature card with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard to play.
Common reanimation, held to creatures of mana value 3 or less so it brings back a Chanter or a Glutton rather than a demon. Four mana for a returned three-drop is fair card advantage in a long game. The flavour makes the point that the Pact does not respect the rest of the dead.

**Black Altar** · Common · Relic (Relic) · 1B · Sacrifice a creature: the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
A cheap sacrifice outlet that turns every creature about to die into a point of drain. It does nothing alone, which keeps it a common, but with Initiates and Restless Dead it becomes a steady leak of the enemy's life. The black altar is the heart of the Pact's worship, and its stone is never dry.

**Gibbet of the Forsworn** · Common · Relic (Relic) · 2B · Tap, pay 1 life: target enemy creature gets -1/-1 until end of turn.
A slow, life-hungry removal engine: 1 life a turn to shrink an enemy creature. It kills x/1s and swings combats, and the life cost makes it a real decision every turn. A gibbet hanging an oathbreaker in the wind is a warning to every man who passes below.

**Bone-Priest of the Pact** · Uncommon · Creature (Human Cultist) · 1B · 1/2 · Sacrifice another creature: draw a card and you lose 1 life.
A two-drop that turns sacrifices into cards, each one costing a point of life. It does nothing without fodder, so it is an uncommon engine for decks built around Initiates and tokens. A priest who reads the future in knucklebones and never runs short of them needs no further comment.

**Wailing Wraith** · Uncommon · Creature (Spirit) · 2B · 2/2 · Whenever this attacks, target enemy creature gets -1/-1 until end of turn.
An on-rate 2/2 for three that shrinks a blocker each time it attacks. It makes the enemy's blocks worse every turn, a nice twist on a simple body. The wail that widens the weak place in every heart is shrink told as despair.

**Veiled Blade of the Pact** · Uncommon · Creature (Human Assassin) · 2B · 2/1 · Deathtouch, dread.
Two Pact keywords on a 2/1 for three. Only Pact creatures and Constructs can block it, and whatever does block it dies, so it hits reliably while staying fragile to any removal. Paying assassins in years taken from their victims is suitably sinister.

**Cinder-Pit Fiend** · Uncommon · Creature (Demon) · 2BB · 4/4 · Dread. At the beginning of your upkeep, you lose 1 life.
A 4/4 dread for four, well above rate, taxed by a life every upkeep. It rewards the player who attacks with it at once and punishes the one who keeps it too long, which is the lesson: the demon serves but is always hungry.

**Tempter of Broken Vows** · Uncommon · Creature (Demon) · 3B · 2/2 · When this enters, the enemy discards a card at random. When this dies, the enemy discards a card at random.
A 2/2 for four that strips a card when it enters and another when it dies. The weak body is the price of a likely three for one, and the enemy gains nothing by killing it. A tempter who never lies but lets you hear what you wanted is the oldest trick of the enemy of souls.

**Blood-Bound Reaver** · Uncommon · Creature (Human Warrior) · 3B · 3/3 · Pay 2 life: this gets +1/+1 until end of turn.
An on-rate 3/3 for four that can grow as long as its owner will bleed for it. The ability has no limit, so it threatens a big hit, but every point costs two life and walks you toward the grave. A warrior who takes every wound on credit is the pact made flesh.

**Grave Summoner** · Uncommon · Creature (Human Necromancer) · 4B · 2/3 · When this enters, return target creature card with mana value 4 or less from your graveyard to play.
Value reanimation on a weak body. It now returns only creatures of mana value 4 or less, as the first set notes suggested, so it cannot bring back a mythic on turn five. The necromancer's line shows the Pact's contempt for the rest of the dead.

**Unmaking Rite** · Uncommon · Spell (Curse) · 2B · Destroy target enemy creature. You lose 2 life.
Unconditional removal for three mana and 2 life, moved down to uncommon as planned. It answers anything, and the life is what makes it fair. Every unmaking costing the maker a little of his own soul states the faith's rule directly.

**Breath of the Ossuary** · Uncommon · Spell (Curse) · 1BB · All creatures get -2/-2 until end of turn.
A symmetric sweeper: every creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn. It wipes out tokens and small creatures on both sides, so the Pact player must build around it, often with death triggers that profit from his own losses. Bones sighing and the living growing lighter is a quiet, eerie image.

**The Debt Comes Due** · Uncommon · Spell (Curse) · 2B · If you have 10 or less life (Bloodied), the enemy player loses 2 life and you gain 2 life. Then the enemy player loses 2 life and you gain 2 life.
Drain 2 for three mana, doubled to drain 4 if you are Bloodied when you cast it. It is the comeback card of the faith: weak when healthy, strong when the price has already been paid. The contract written very small is the faith's whole method.

**Chalice of Black Vows** · Uncommon · Relic (Relic) · 2B · At the beginning of your upkeep, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
A drain clock: two points of swing each turn for three mana. It is slow and does nothing to the board, so it fits uncommon. A chalice that is never empty and whose contents no one asks about inverts the cup of the altar, and the inversion is meant to look evil.

**Knife of the Black Tithe** · Uncommon · Relic (Relic) · 2B · Sacrifice a creature: target enemy creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn.
A sacrifice outlet that turns your creatures into shrink effects. It makes chump blocks into removal and gives Restless Dead tokens a second job. The tithe of the pit is one life for the knife and one for the curse, and the flavour says so.

**Censer of Bitter Ash** · Uncommon · Relic (Relic) · 2B · Tap, pay 2 and 2 life: the enemy discards a card at random.
Repeatable hand attack at a steep price: two mana and 2 life for each random discard. It grinds out long games and is too slow to matter in short ones. The censer mocks the incense of the sanctuary, carrying thoughts away instead of prayers up, a profanation that shows its evil plainly.

**Oathbreaker Knight** · Rare · Creature (Human Knight) · 2B · 3/3 · As long as you have 10 or less life (Bloodied), this gets +2/+2 and has dread and deathtouch.
The Bloodied build-around. A 3/3 for three becomes a 5/5 with dread and deathtouch once you are at 10 life or less, which is a real threat that only Pact creatures can block and that kills them when they do. He swore to guard the weak and sold the oath for strength, the clearest picture of the broken vow.

**Dream-Thief Fiend** · Rare · Creature (Demon) · 2BB · 2/2 · Dread. Whenever this attacks, the enemy discards a card at random.
A dread 2/2 for four that strips a card each time it attacks. It is a slow lock that every turn it survives makes worse, and its small body leaves it open to removal, which keeps it fair at rare. Forgetting your plan by morning is a quiet horror that fits a demon of the night.

**Marquis of the Wailing Pit** · Rare · Creature (Demon) · 3BB · 5/5 · Dread. When this enters, you lose 3 life.
A 5/5 dread demon, now at five mana and 3 life instead of four mana and 4 life, because the first version was too efficient when ahead. It is still a strong aggressive rare, and the life keeps the price felt. Marquis is a rank from the old demonologies, and presenting his bill at the door is the Pact in one line.

**Hollow-Crowned Lich** · Rare · Creature (Lich) · 3BB · 3/5 · Deathtouch. At the beginning of your upkeep, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
A durable defensive engine: a deathtouch 3/5 that drains a point every upkeep. Its stats are about par, and the drain makes it a rare that wins long games if left alone. The hollow crown is what sin pays: everything traded, nothing gained.

**Bone-Lord of the Ossuary** · Rare · Creature (Skeleton) · 4B · 3/3 · Other Skeletons you control get +1/+1. Pay B, sacrifice another creature: create a 2/2 Restless Dead Zombie token.
The Skeleton build-around (since 1.25; he was a Zombie lord before and is now redrawn as a crowned skeleton). He strengthens the other Skeletons and can turn any creature, for B and a sacrifice, into a 2/2 Restless Dead Zombie, which feeds the Zombies' payoffs instead of his own anthem. The anthem is restricted to Skeletons, so it does not tread on the Church's team-wide anthems. A lord whose walls are lined with the skulls of his kneeling subjects shows the rule of the pit.

**Profane Resurrection** · Rare · Spell (Necromancy) · 2B · Return target creature card from your graveyard to play. You lose 4 life.
Unrestricted reanimation for three mana and 4 life. It can bring back a demon lord, so the life price is high and the card sits at rare. The flavour calls it a mockery of the true rising, which keeps the necromancy plainly evil.

**Night of Unmaking** · Rare · Spell (Curse) · 3BB · Destroy all creatures.
Destroy all creatures for five mana, the Pact's sweeper. It is the faith of unconditional destruction, so it gets the true wrath, and its owner loses his own board too. The Pact counting a silent field as victory says what it values.

**Rite of the Emptied Mind** · Rare · Spell (Curse) · 1BB · The enemy discards three cards at random. You lose 3 life.
Three random discards for three mana and 3 life, a heavy hand attack that can empty a player's options in one stroke. The randomness and the life keep it from being oppressive. The order of loss in the flavour, friends then prayers then the reason for prayer, is the path of despair.

**Ledger of the Damned** · Rare · Relic (Relic) · 3B · Tap: draw a card and you lose 1 life.
The repeatable draw engine, kept at rare as the spec asks. A card a turn for 1 life is strong, but it does nothing to the board and it drains its owner steadily into Bloodied range. Every page a soul, every soul overdue: the Pact as a moneylender of the damned.

**Belzareth, Prince of Cinders** · Mythic · Creature (Demon Lord) · 4BB · 5/5 · Dread. When this enters, destroy target enemy creature and you lose 3 life.
The faith's bomb, rebalanced. He keeps his removal on entry, loses the death drain, trades flying for dread, and now costs 3 life when he arrives. A 5/5 evasive body plus a kill is still a mythic swing, but the price is real. The name echoes Beelzebub, and the earth forgetting it was ever green shows him as blight, not glamour.

**Malgrith, Lich of the Black Ledger** · Mythic · Creature (Lich) · 4BB · 5/5 · When this enters, return target creature card with mana value 4 or less from your graveyard to play. At the beginning of your upkeep, draw a card and you lose 1 life.
The long-game mythic: a 5/5 for six that brings back a creature of mana value 4 or less and then draws a card every upkeep for a point of life. The reanimation limit means he cannot return himself or Belzareth, so he is splashy but not endless. A lich who outlives his debtors and collects from their sons is the pact passed down the generations.

**The Black Contract** · Mythic · Relic (Pact) · 2BB · Enemy creatures get -1/-1. At the beginning of your upkeep, you lose 1 life.
A one-sided -1/-1 to every enemy creature, paid for with a life every turn. It kills tokens and x/1s on sight and shrinks everything else for as long as you can afford it, which makes it the most dramatic card in the faith while still putting its owner on a clock. Sign, and your enemies wither; sign, and so do you.

### Tribal cards (1.23)

The Pact's two tribes are the Skeletons and the Demons, and they play in opposite directions. The Skeletons are cheap, wide and stubborn: bony soldiers that leave Restless Dead behind, and payoffs that count heads (a +1/+0 drummer, a growing Bone-Warden, a shrink curse that scales with the host, the Bone-Lord's anthem) and finally hand the whole host dread under the Barrow-King. The Demons are few, large and expensive in life: a cost cutter, enter triggers that reward each new fiend, a lord that taxes its owner for every demon in play, and a mythic duke whose court grows more dangerous with each arrival. (In 1.23 the first tribe was "Undead", an umbrella over zombies, skeletons, spirits and liches; it proved too broad and too strong, so 1.25 split it: Skeletons became the main tribe with eight members, Zombies became the small third tribe, and Spirits and Liches keep their own names and count for neither.) Grave Chill and Unmaking Rite become instants: a shrink that answers an attack and a kill that costs life are the natural tricks of the faith, and both stay at their old cost because neither outclasses the instant removal of the era.

**Lych-Gate Shambler** · Common · Creature (Zombie) · B · 1/1 · This gets +1/+0 for each other Zombie you control.
The Zombie one-drop (re-aimed from Undead in 1.25): a 1/1 that hits harder the more Zombies and Restless Dead tokens stand beside it. Only power grows, so it stays fragile and trades with anything once the board is cleared. The lych-gate is where a parish coffin rests before burial, which makes it the right door for the dead to come back through.

**Cackling Imp** · Common · Creature (Demon Imp) · 1B · 1/2 · Whenever another Demon enters under your control, target enemy creature gets -1/-1 until end of turn.
A cheap Demon that rewards every later Demon with a small shrink, enough to kill a token or win a combat. It does nothing on its own, which suits a common two-drop. Imps are the petty servants of greater fiends, and their glee at another's suffering is the oldest picture of them.

**Bone-Rattle Picket** · Common · Creature (Skeleton) · 2B · 2/2 · When this dies, create a 1/1 Restless Dead Zombie token.
An on-rate Skeleton that leaves a Zombie token behind, so it serves both tribes: it counts for the Skeleton lords while alive, then for the Shambler and the Corpse-Bell, and it feeds every sacrifice outlet. It keeps the rule that the Pact raises the dead only from death or sacrifice. A dead sentry still keeping his post at the barrow is the Skeletons' stubbornness in one figure.

**Sulphur Brute** · Common · Creature (Demon) · 2B · 4/3 · When this enters, you lose 2 life.
The honest common Demon: a full point above rate for three mana, paid for in life. It triggers the Imp, the Herald's discount and the Duke, and it walks its owner toward Bloodied. A fiend coming up through a farmhouse cellar keeps the horror close to home.

**Chill of the Charnel Host** · Common · Spell (Curse) · 1B · Target enemy creature gets -1/-1 until end of turn for each Skeleton you control.
The Skeleton removal: weak with one skeleton, a kill with three or four. It is an instant so it can answer an attack or ambush a blocker, and it fizzles with no Skeletons in play, which keeps it honest. The cold of a single dead hand multiplied by a host is the image of the whole tribe.

**Tithe of the Pit** · Common · Spell (Pact) · 2B · Draw a card for each Demon you control. You lose 1 life for each Demon you control.
The Demon card draw, always paired with life as the Pact's draw must be. With one Demon it is weak, with two it is a fair Bargain in Blood, and with three it refills the hand at a real price. Every whisper of a fiend is billed to the master who keeps him.

**Marsh-Light Shade** · Common · Creature (Spirit) · 3B · 3/2 · Dread (can only be blocked by Pact creatures and Constructs).
A common evasive Spirit between the Hound and the Wight: four mana, a point under rate for dread. Since 1.25 it belongs to neither tribe, a plain evasive clock for any Pact deck. The will-o'-the-wisp that leads travellers off the causeway into the fen is old folklore of the drowned dead.

**Drummer of the Dead March** · Uncommon · Creature (Skeleton) · 2B · 2/2 · Other Skeletons you control get +1/+0.
The Skeleton lord at uncommon, held to +1/+0 so the rare Bone-Lord keeps the stronger anthem. It turns a column of bony soldiers into a real attack (his marching column is now drawn all of skeletons) and is a priority target, as a lord should be. The danse macabre led by a drumming skeleton is one of the most familiar late medieval images of death.

**Herald of the Burning Gate** · Uncommon · Creature (Demon) · 2B · 2/2 · Your Demon creature cards cost 1 less to play.
The Demon enabler: a weak body that brings the big fiends a turn early. Demons already cost life as well as mana, so cutting the mana is where the tribe gains speed. The herald who holds the gate while something larger comes through tells the player exactly what he is for.

**Gravecaller Bone-Warden** · Uncommon · Creature (Skeleton) · 3B · 3/3 · Whenever another Skeleton enters under your control, this gets +1/+1 for the rest of the duel.
An on-rate 3/3 that grows permanently with every Skeleton that arrives, reanimated ones included (Zombie tokens no longer feed it, which keeps it from snowballing off a single sacrifice outlet). It rewards the slow, wide game of the tribe and becomes a must-answer threat if left alone. Renamed from Gravecaller Ghast and redrawn as a helmed skeleton in 1.25; the dead feeding their cold into one warden fits the Pact's arithmetic.

**Brimstone Exhalation** · Uncommon · Spell (Curse) · 2BB · All enemy creatures get -1/-1 until end of turn for each Demon you control.
A one-sided sweeper that only works for a player committed to Demons: with one it clears tokens, with two or three it clears a board. Four mana and the need for a board in play keep it from being a plain wrath. Sulphur and choking fumes are the smell of hell in every medieval vision of it.

**Corpse-Bell of the Barrow** · Uncommon · Relic (Relic) · 2B · Whenever a Zombie you control dies, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
The Zombie drain engine (re-aimed in 1.25, and in 1.27 turned into a death knell with the new ally death trigger). Every Zombie that falls in a trade, to the Glutton's hunger or as a Restless Dead token tips the race by two, about as often as the old "enters" version did, so the relic is strong only in a deck built for it. A bell cast to ring the dead to rest and recast to wake them is a plain profanation, and the flavour says so.

**Barrow-King of the Hollow Hills** · Rare · Creature (Skeleton Lord) · 3BB · 4/4 · Other Skeletons you control have dread (they can only be blocked by Pact creatures and Constructs).
The Skeleton build-around (redrawn in 1.25 as the crowned skeleton of the old king, his host all bones): the whole host becomes evasive, which turns a wide board into lethal damage against most faiths. He gives nothing to himself and is a 4/4 for five, a point under rate, so removing him ends the threat. The old king riding out of the hollow hill with his dead host is English and Northern barrow lore.

**Brimstone Marshal** · Rare · Creature (Demon) · 3BB · 4/4 · Other Demons you control get +1/+1. When this enters, you lose 1 life for each Demon you control.
The Demon lord. The anthem is restricted to Demons, so it does not tread on the Church's team anthems, and its price grows with the very board it strengthens. It is a strong rare that punishes a player already low on life. A marshal of hell drilling ranks of lesser fiends is the infernal mirror of an earthly army.

**Azgaroth, Duke of the Ninth Pit** · Mythic · Creature (Demon Lord) · 4BB · 5/5 · Dread. Other Demons you control have deathtouch (any damage they deal to a creature destroys it). Whenever another Demon enters under your control, the enemy discards a card at random. When this enters, you lose 3 life.
The Demon mythic. He is a 5/5 dread for six that costs 3 life, makes every other Demon a deathtouch threat and strips a card with each new fiend, so his court snowballs. He does nothing for himself beyond his body, and a single removal spell ends the court, which keeps him splashy but answerable. Dukes are a rank from the old demonologies, and a court of debtors who never leave is the Pact's idea of nobility.

### Third tribe (1.25)

The Pact's third tribe is the Zombies: the shrouded dead of the plague pits and the ghouls that dig them up. Where the Skeletons are a disciplined host with lords, the Zombies are a small rabble with a few synergies built on what the Pact already does: they die cheaply and drain as they go, they turn into Restless Dead tokens that count for the tribe, and they bring the small dead back out of the grave. The payoffs are the Lych-Gate Shambler's kinship and the Corpse-Bell's drain (both re-aimed from Undead), a drain spell that counts Zombies and one modest rare lord. Members: Famished Ghoul, Charnel Glutton, Lych-Gate Shambler and the four new creatures, seven in all, plus every Restless Dead token, which the Picket, the Bone-Walker and the Bone-Lord all make. All Restless Dead are Zombies and still come only from death or sacrifice.

**Plague-Pit Shambler** · Common · Creature (Zombie) · 1B · 2/1 · When this dies, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
The Zombie two-drop and the tribe's fodder: an on-rate 2/1 that drains on the way out, so it is happy to trade, chump or go onto the Black Altar. It is the Pact Initiate one size up. A dead man climbing out of the lime of a plague pit is the dread of every town that lived through the pestilence.

**Gravepit Ghoul** · Common · Creature (Zombie) · 3B · 2/2 · When this enters, return target creature card from your graveyard to your hand.
The grave return at common, since 1.27 a true gravedigger: a 2/2 for four, two points under rate, that puts any creature card of your choice back in your hand. It no longer puts the creature straight into play, which pays for lifting the mana value limit; the card is still a plain two for one in the style of the era. A ghoul hauling a dead man up out of his broken coffin is the necromancy of the Pact at its lowest.

**Plague-Cart Drover** · Uncommon · Creature (Zombie) · 2B · 2/2 · When this dies, create two 1/1 Restless Dead Zombie tokens.
