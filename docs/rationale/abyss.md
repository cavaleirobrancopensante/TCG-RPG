## The Black Covenant (B)

The Covenant is the faith of the pact: everything it does has a price. It pays life for oversized creatures and cards, sacrifices its own followers to fuel its altars, and answers enemies with unconditional destruction, shrinking curses and hand attack. Its creatures carry deathtouch and dread instead of wings, and the Bloodied condition rewards the player who has already paid, until the debt finally comes due.

**Covenant Initiate** · Common · Creature (Human Cultist) · B · 1/1 · When this dies, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
The cheapest body in the faith and the first piece of fodder for every sacrifice outlet. A 1/1 for one is below rate, but the drain on death means it is never a wasted card, whether it chump blocks or goes onto the Black Altar. The flavour states the Covenant's logic plainly: even a follower's death belongs to his master.

**Pit Imp** · Common · Creature (Demon Imp) · B · 1/1 · Dread (can only be blocked by Covenant creatures and Constructs).
A one-drop with dread, so it chips in a point every turn against any deck that has no Covenant creatures or Constructs. One power keeps it harmless as a threat on its own, which is right for a common. Imps are the smallest servants of the pit, and the flavour keeps them petty and mean rather than charming.

**Blood-Oath Thrall** · Common · Creature (Human Cultist) · 1B · 3/2 · When this enters, you lose 1 life.
The pact in its simplest form: a 3/2 for two, a full point above rate, paid for with 1 life. It is the faith's honest aggressive common and an early step toward Bloodied. The fingertip of blood and the debt that came due later make clear that the deal is a trap.

**Tomb Crawler** · Common · Creature (Zombie) · 1B · 1/1 · Deathtouch (any damage this deals to a creature destroys it).
A cheap deterrent. Nothing large wants to attack into a deathtouch blocker, so this 1/1 for two holds the ground far above its size. It shows the Covenant's habit of destroying what is greater than itself.

**Famished Ghoul** · Common · Creature (Zombie) · 1B · 2/2 · As long as you have 10 or less life (Bloodied), this gets +1/+1.
The common introduction to Bloodied: an on-rate 2/2 for two that becomes a 3/3 once you have paid enough life. It rewards the player who has been spending life freely without punishing anyone early. A ghoul that smells you better as you near the grave is exactly what the condition means.

**Hellmaw Hound** · Common · Creature (Demon Hound) · 2B · 2/2 · Dread (can only be blocked by Covenant creatures and Constructs).
The common dread creature, a 2/2 for three with evasion priced like a flier. It replaces the old flying shade, since the Covenant no longer flies, and gives the faith a steady evasive clock at common. Honest dogs fleeing from it while only the damned can stand in its way is dread told as flavour.

**Grave Chanter** · Common · Creature (Human Cultist) · 2B · 2/3 · When this enters, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
A 2/3 for three with a one point drain on arrival. The body is a little under rate and the drain makes it up, so it is a steady common that tips races and blocks small attackers. Singing hymns backwards is the traditional inversion of sacred worship and marks the cultist as plainly diabolic.

**Charnel Glutton** · Common · Creature (Zombie) · 2B · 2/2 · Sacrifice another creature: this gets +2/+2 until end of turn.
The common sacrifice outlet. A plain 2/2 for three that can eat a doomed blocker or a Restless Dead token to win a combat or push damage through. It teaches the faith's habit of spending its own creatures without being a combo engine, because the bonus only lasts the turn.

**Blight-Sower Cultist** · Common · Creature (Human Cultist) · 3B · 2/2 · When this enters, target enemy creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn.
The common shrink creature: a 2/2 for four that kills an x/2 or wins a combat when it arrives. The body is under rate because the effect is a real two for one against small creatures. A cultist scattering ash and calling it a blessing is a mockery of the farmer's prayer, which shows the faith for what it is.

**Ossuary Ghoul** · Common · Creature (Zombie) · 3B · 3/3 · When this dies, create a 1/1 Restless Dead Zombie token.
A 3/3 for four, a point under rate, that leaves a Restless Dead behind. It trades for two cards and feeds sacrifice outlets, and it follows the rule that the Covenant only raises the dead from death or sacrifice. Ossuaries are bone houses, and bones that keep walking after the body breaks are the faith's grim joke.

**Pit-Chained Fiend** · Common · Creature (Demon) · 3B · 5/4 · When this enters, you lose 2 life.
The common top end for aggressive decks: a 5/4 for four, well above rate, for 2 life. It is the bigger brother of the Thrall and pushes its owner toward Bloodied. The chain is for show, and the flavour reminds the player that what really binds is the price.

**Barrow Wight** · Common · Creature (Spirit) · 4B · 4/3 · Dread (can only be blocked by Covenant creatures and Constructs).
A 4/3 with dread for five, the common finisher. It sits a point under a vanilla five-drop to pay for the evasion, which keeps it fair while still closing games against most faiths. Wights clutching their grave gold are old folklore, and its greed suits the Covenant.

**Grave Chill** · Common · Spell (Curse) · B · Target enemy creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn.
One mana for -2/-2 until end of turn: it kills x/2s outright or turns a combat. It is the cheapest removal in the faith and is limited by toughness, so big threats shrug it off. The cold that does not stay in the crypt is the shrink mechanic told as weather.

**Wither the Living** · Common · Spell (Curse) · 1B · Destroy target enemy creature with power 2 or less.
Unconditional destroy belongs to the Covenant, but at common and two mana it is held to small creatures. It is efficient early removal that cannot answer bombs. The line about borrowed flesh is a reminder that the faith treats life as something to be repossessed.

**Bargain in Blood** · Common · Spell (Pact) · 1B · Draw two cards. You lose 2 life.
Draw two for two mana and 2 life, the Covenant's only way to draw: by paying. It is a little cheaper than the plain draw spells of the Stoa because life is a real resource here. The scribe who never mentions the interest is the pact in miniature.

**Siphon of Ashes** · Common · Spell (Curse) · 1B · The enemy player loses 2 life and you gain 2 life.
A four point swing for two mana. It is reach for drain decks and does nothing to the board, which keeps it fair at common, and it lets a Bloodied player climb back out if he needs to. The cracked chalice deliberately profanes a sacred vessel, showing the Covenant's mockery of holy things as the evil it is.

**Dread Whisper** · Common · Spell (Curse) · 2B · The enemy discards two cards at random.
Two cards at random for three mana, the classic common hand attack. It was a little cheap at two mana in the first set, so it moves to three and to common. Undoing every plan with a single word in the night is the tempter's work.

**Unquiet Grave** · Common · Spell (Necromancy) · 3B · Return your best creature card with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard to play.
Common reanimation, held to creatures of mana value 3 or less so it brings back a Chanter or a Glutton rather than a demon. Four mana for a returned three-drop is fair card advantage in a long game. The flavour makes the point that the Covenant does not respect the rest of the dead.

**Black Altar** · Common · Relic · 1B · Sacrifice a creature: the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
A cheap sacrifice outlet that turns every creature about to die into a point of drain. It does nothing alone, which keeps it a common, but with Initiates and Restless Dead it becomes a steady leak of the enemy's life. The black altar is the heart of the Covenant's worship, and its stone is never dry.

**Gibbet of the Forsworn** · Common · Relic · 2B · Tap, pay 1 life: target enemy creature gets -1/-1 until end of turn.
A slow, life-hungry removal engine: 1 life a turn to shrink an enemy creature. It kills x/1s and swings combats, and the life cost makes it a real decision every turn. A gibbet hanging an oathbreaker in the wind is a warning to every man who passes below.

**Bone-Priest of the Covenant** · Uncommon · Creature (Human Cultist) · 1B · 1/2 · Sacrifice another creature: draw a card and you lose 1 life.
A two-drop that turns sacrifices into cards, each one costing a point of life. It does nothing without fodder, so it is an uncommon engine for decks built around Initiates and tokens. A priest who reads the future in knucklebones and never runs short of them needs no further comment.

**Wailing Wraith** · Uncommon · Creature (Spirit) · 2B · 2/2 · Whenever this attacks, target enemy creature gets -1/-1 until end of turn.
An on-rate 2/2 for three that shrinks a blocker each time it attacks. It makes the enemy's blocks worse every turn, a nice twist on a simple body. The wail that widens the weak place in every heart is shrink told as despair.

**Veiled Blade of the Covenant** · Uncommon · Creature (Human Assassin) · 2B · 2/1 · Deathtouch, dread.
Two Covenant keywords on a 2/1 for three. Only Covenant creatures and Constructs can block it, and whatever does block it dies, so it hits reliably while staying fragile to any removal. Paying assassins in years taken from their victims is suitably sinister.

**Cinder-Pit Fiend** · Uncommon · Creature (Demon) · 2BB · 4/4 · Dread. At the beginning of your upkeep, you lose 1 life.
A 4/4 dread for four, well above rate, taxed by a life every upkeep. It rewards the player who attacks with it at once and punishes the one who keeps it too long, which is the lesson: the demon serves but is always hungry.

**Tempter of Broken Vows** · Uncommon · Creature (Demon) · 3B · 2/2 · When this enters, the enemy discards a card at random. When this dies, the enemy discards a card at random.
A 2/2 for four that strips a card when it enters and another when it dies. The weak body is the price of a likely three for one, and the enemy gains nothing by killing it. A tempter who never lies but lets you hear what you wanted is the oldest trick of the enemy of souls.

**Blood-Bound Reaver** · Uncommon · Creature (Human Warrior) · 3B · 3/3 · Pay 2 life: this gets +1/+1 until end of turn.
An on-rate 3/3 for four that can grow as long as its owner will bleed for it. The ability has no limit, so it threatens a big hit, but every point costs two life and walks you toward the grave. A warrior who takes every wound on credit is the pact made flesh.

**Grave Summoner** · Uncommon · Creature (Human Necromancer) · 4B · 2/3 · When this enters, return your best creature card with mana value 4 or less from your graveyard to play.
Value reanimation on a weak body. It now returns only creatures of mana value 4 or less, as the first set notes suggested, so it cannot bring back a mythic on turn five. The necromancer's line shows the Covenant's contempt for the rest of the dead.

**Unmaking Rite** · Uncommon · Spell (Curse) · 2B · Destroy target enemy creature. You lose 2 life.
Unconditional removal for three mana and 2 life, moved down to uncommon as planned. It answers anything, and the life is what makes it fair. Every unmaking costing the maker a little of his own soul states the faith's rule directly.

**Breath of the Ossuary** · Uncommon · Spell (Curse) · 1BB · All creatures get -2/-2 until end of turn.
A symmetric sweeper: every creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn. It wipes out tokens and small creatures on both sides, so the Covenant player must build around it, often with death triggers that profit from his own losses. Bones sighing and the living growing lighter is a quiet, eerie image.

**The Debt Comes Due** · Uncommon · Spell (Curse) · 2B · If you have 10 or less life (Bloodied), the enemy player loses 2 life and you gain 2 life. Then the enemy player loses 2 life and you gain 2 life.
Drain 2 for three mana, doubled to drain 4 if you are Bloodied when you cast it. It is the comeback card of the faith: weak when healthy, strong when the price has already been paid. The contract written very small is the faith's whole method.

**Chalice of Black Vows** · Uncommon · Relic · 2B · At the beginning of your upkeep, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
A drain clock: two points of swing each turn for three mana. It is slow and does nothing to the board, so it fits uncommon. A chalice that is never empty and whose contents no one asks about inverts the cup of the altar, and the inversion is meant to look evil.

**Knife of the Black Tithe** · Uncommon · Relic · 2B · Sacrifice a creature: target enemy creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn.
A sacrifice outlet that turns your creatures into shrink effects. It makes chump blocks into removal and gives Restless Dead tokens a second job. The tithe of the pit is one life for the knife and one for the curse, and the flavour says so.

**Censer of Bitter Ash** · Uncommon · Relic · 2B · Tap, pay 2 and 2 life: the enemy discards a card at random.
Repeatable hand attack at a steep price: two mana and 2 life for each random discard. It grinds out long games and is too slow to matter in short ones. The censer mocks the incense of the sanctuary, carrying thoughts away instead of prayers up, a profanation that shows its evil plainly.

**Oathbreaker Knight** · Rare · Creature (Human Knight) · 2B · 3/3 · As long as you have 10 or less life (Bloodied), this gets +2/+2 and has dread and deathtouch.
The Bloodied build-around. A 3/3 for three becomes a 5/5 with dread and deathtouch once you are at 10 life or less, which is a real threat that only Covenant creatures can block and that kills them when they do. He swore to guard the weak and sold the oath for strength, the clearest picture of the broken vow.

**Dream-Thief Fiend** · Rare · Creature (Demon) · 2BB · 2/2 · Dread. Whenever this attacks, the enemy discards a card at random.
A dread 2/2 for four that strips a card each time it attacks. It is a slow lock that every turn it survives makes worse, and its small body leaves it open to removal, which keeps it fair at rare. Forgetting your plan by morning is a quiet horror that fits a demon of the night.

**Marquis of the Wailing Pit** · Rare · Creature (Demon) · 3BB · 5/5 · Dread. When this enters, you lose 3 life.
A 5/5 dread demon, now at five mana and 3 life instead of four mana and 4 life, because the first version was too efficient when ahead. It is still a strong aggressive rare, and the life keeps the price felt. Marquis is a rank from the old demonologies, and presenting his bill at the door is the Covenant in one line.

**Hollow-Crowned Lich** · Rare · Creature (Lich) · 3BB · 3/5 · Deathtouch. At the beginning of your upkeep, the enemy player loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.
A durable defensive engine: a deathtouch 3/5 that drains a point every upkeep. Its stats are about par, and the drain makes it a rare that wins long games if left alone. The hollow crown is what sin pays: everything traded, nothing gained.

**Bone-Lord of the Ossuary** · Rare · Creature (Zombie) · 4B · 3/3 · Other Zombies you control get +1/+1. Pay B, sacrifice another creature: create a 2/2 Restless Dead Zombie token.
The Zombie build-around. It strengthens the other Zombies and can turn any creature, for B and a sacrifice, into a 2/2 Restless Dead that becomes a 3/3 under his rule. The anthem is restricted to Zombies, so it does not tread on the Church's team-wide anthems. A lord whose walls are lined with the skulls of his kneeling subjects shows the rule of the pit.

**Profane Resurrection** · Rare · Spell (Necromancy) · 2B · Return your best creature card from your graveyard to play. You lose 4 life.
Unrestricted reanimation for three mana and 4 life. It can bring back a demon lord, so the life price is high and the card sits at rare. The flavour calls it a mockery of the true rising, which keeps the necromancy plainly evil.

**Night of Unmaking** · Rare · Spell (Curse) · 3BB · Destroy all creatures.
Destroy all creatures for five mana, the Covenant's sweeper. It is the faith of unconditional destruction, so it gets the true wrath, and its owner loses his own board too. The Covenant counting a silent field as victory says what it values.

**Rite of the Emptied Mind** · Rare · Spell (Curse) · 1BB · The enemy discards three cards at random. You lose 3 life.
Three random discards for three mana and 3 life, a heavy hand attack that can empty a player's options in one stroke. The randomness and the life keep it from being oppressive. The order of loss in the flavour, friends then prayers then the reason for prayer, is the path of despair.

**Ledger of the Damned** · Rare · Relic · 3B · Tap: draw a card and you lose 1 life.
The repeatable draw engine, kept at rare as the spec asks. A card a turn for 1 life is strong, but it does nothing to the board and it drains its owner steadily into Bloodied range. Every page a soul, every soul overdue: the Covenant as a moneylender of the damned.

**Belzareth, Prince of Cinders** · Mythic · Creature (Demon Lord) · 4BB · 5/5 · Dread. When this enters, destroy target enemy creature and you lose 3 life.
The faith's bomb, rebalanced. He keeps his removal on entry, loses the death drain, trades flying for dread, and now costs 3 life when he arrives. A 5/5 evasive body plus a kill is still a mythic swing, but the price is real. The name echoes Beelzebub, and the earth forgetting it was ever green shows him as blight, not glamour.

**Malgrith, Lich of the Black Ledger** · Mythic · Creature (Lich) · 4BB · 5/5 · When this enters, return your best creature card with mana value 4 or less from your graveyard to play. At the beginning of your upkeep, draw a card and you lose 1 life.
The long-game mythic: a 5/5 for six that brings back a creature of mana value 4 or less and then draws a card every upkeep for a point of life. The reanimation limit means he cannot return himself or Belzareth, so he is splashy but not endless. A lich who outlives his debtors and collects from their sons is the pact passed down the generations.

**The Black Contract** · Mythic · Relic (Pact) · 2BB · Enemy creatures get -1/-1. At the beginning of your upkeep, you lose 1 life.
A one-sided -1/-1 to every enemy creature, paid for with a life every turn. It kills tokens and x/1s on sight and shrinks everything else for as long as you can afford it, which makes it the most dramatic card in the faith while still putting its owner on a clock. Sign, and your enemies wither; sign, and so do you.
