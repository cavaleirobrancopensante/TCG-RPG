# Translation glossary (Brazilian Portuguese / Spanish)

Register: a traditional, Catholic-minded medieval fantasy card game. Dignified, warm, a little archaic
where it suits (card flavour), plain and short in buttons and menus. The Holy Church is always treated
with reverence. Angels are masculine. Soldiers/knights/fighters are men. No gender-neutral modern forms.

names (Father Anselm → keep the name but translate the title separately), Latin (Lumen Ecclesiae, Deus Vult,
Victoria, Clades, Aurea), "TCG · RPG", symbols (♥ ◉ ✦ ✉ ⚔ ★ ✗ ▶ ♪), and any card/creature proper name like
"Belzareth", "Cernoth", "Vessaria", "Aristeon" (translate only the epithet after the comma).
